## Tiny Acid Jam

<video autoplay loop muted playsinline poster="../assets/images/acidcandy-poster.webp" style="width:640px;max-width:100%"><source src="../assets/images/acidcandy.mp4" type="video/mp4"></video>

A little acid box that fits in your hand. Five machines live on one colour-cartridge nav
strip — two TB-303 lines, a TR-808 and a TR-909 drum machine, plus a master panel — and they
all run on one transport. Tap a cartridge to focus it; tap its LED to mute it from anywhere.
Squelchy acid line, glides, accents, ties, the whole lot, and a Devil Fish mod you can flip on
and off.

### How it works

Every voice is honest. The 303s, the 808 and the 909 are the *same code* the standalone
dreamengine instruments use, shared from the engine so they can't quietly drift apart. Each
303 face has the classic knobs — cutoff, resonance, envelope, decay, accent, riding a real
diode-ladder filter — and a hidden Devil Fish page with a sub-osc, a second decay, slide time
and filter tracking.

You build patterns right on the little green screen: arm a flag — note, accent, slide, tie,
octave — and paint it onto the sixteen note-bars. Drag a bar up or down to pitch it, snapped
to a minor-pentatonic so whatever you do stays musical. Program the 808 and the 909, add
swing, distortion and reverb sends, and ride the master panel for the filter, the PCF, or a
bit of bitcrush and gate on the way to the drop. Or just hit **GEN** and let it invent a line
for you.

### Where it came from

It began as a single screen inside a bigger "acid rack" experiment. This is that screen
becoming its own thing — a pocket acid house machine for when the mood strikes, no mic, no
gear, no accounts. It's also out as a proper app: **Tiny Acid Jam** on the App Store, the same
groovebox, in your actual pocket. The web version here is the whole thing to mess about with
in the browser first.
